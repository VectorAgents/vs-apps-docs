The customer in `agent-studio` stays "a customer", unnamed on purpose.

## Naming rules

A new persona follows these rules:

- The name has no tech meaning.
- The initial differs from every other persona in the table.
- The name carries no job title, unless the role is a Vector Space role with a glossary entry (Lena, organization admin).

## Figures

Figure text never names a persona. A figure stays generic: it says "a member".
